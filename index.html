POUCHRUN

Squeektoshi runs the real wheel. You run the track beside him.

  • His RPM is your speed. The faster he runs, the faster the track and the bigger your multiplier.
  • Fill your cheek pouch with seeds, then stash them at a burrow to bank points. Crash and you drop everything unbanked.
  • Every real Squeek block the wheel completes drops a golden Block Seed onto your track.
  • When he sleeps, night falls. Seeds are worth double, but you only see what your lantern shows.
